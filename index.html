01Quick introduction

More than just making things look good.

I’m Theresa — a Senior Graphic & UI/UX Designer who treats every screen as a business decision. Over 7+ years I’ve created key visuals, brand identities, social content and motion, and designed enterprise websites and apps for banks, manufacturers, entertainment and retail brands, often with Indonesian and Japanese teams in the same room. I like asking “why does this button get ignored?” — because the answer usually changes the brief.
